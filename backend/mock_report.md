# GlobalCorp Q4 2026 Business Review

## Executive Summary
- Revenue grew 18% year-over-year to $128M in Q4 2026.
- Net profit margin improved to 14.2% after tax.
- Customer churn dropped from 3.1% to 2.4%.
- The Asia-Pacific region delivered the strongest growth at +32%.

## Revenue by Region
- Americas: $58M (+9% YoY), driven by enterprise renewals.
- EMEA: $34M (+14% YoY), led by the new Frankfurt data center.
- Asia-Pacific: $36M (+32% YoY), powered by mobile payments adoption.
- Rest of World: $4M, flat.

## Product Performance
- Flagship Platform: $61M revenue, 74% gross margin.
- Mobile Payments Suite: $39M revenue, 68% gross margin.
- Data Intelligence: $28M revenue, 61% gross margin.

## Key Initiatives
- Launched AI-powered revenue forecasting in Q3, adopted by 41% of enterprise customers.
- Expanded partner ecosystem to 280 certified partners.
- Reduced infrastructure cost per transaction by 22%.

## Outlook for 2027
- Target revenue of $165M (+29% YoY).
- Expand Asia-Pacific sales team by 40%.
- Launch self-service analytics for mid-market customers.
- Maintain net profit margin above 14%.

## Risks
- Currency volatility in EMEA and APAC markets.
- Enterprise spending softness in the Americas.
- Regulatory changes around data residency.

## Ask
- Increase marketing investment in APAC to $12M.
- Approve 40 new headcount for the APAC sales team.
- Fund the self-service analytics launch in Q2 2027.
