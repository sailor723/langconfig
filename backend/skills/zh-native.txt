# Skills that were WRITTEN in Chinese — no English original exists.

These are not translations: there is nothing in `backend/skills/en-originals/` to be a translation of, and
pretending otherwise would mean inventing an English source. They are listed so the three states stay
distinguishable: translated (has an archived original), authored in Chinese (this file), still English
(`pending-zh.txt`).

Every skill must be in exactly one of the three, and `backend/tests/test_skill_translations.py` checks it.
