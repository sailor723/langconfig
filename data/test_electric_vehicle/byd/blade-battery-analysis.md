# BYD Blade Battery Competitive Analysis

BYD's Blade Battery is the primary competitor to Tesla's 4680.

- **Chemistry**: LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) vs NCA
- **Safety**: Passes nail penetration test with no thermal runaway
- **Cost**: 30% cheaper per kWh than NCA alternatives
- **Range**: 700km (CLTC) in BYD Seal

BYD sold 3.0M EVs in 2025, maintaining #1 global EV seller position.
