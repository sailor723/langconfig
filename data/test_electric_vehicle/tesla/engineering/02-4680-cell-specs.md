# Tesla 4680 Battery Cell Specifications

The 4680 battery cell represents a paradigm shift in EV battery technology.

- **Energy Density**: 300 Wh/kg (vs 2170's 260 Wh/kg)
- **Production Cost**: 56% reduction per kWh
- **Range**: 50% improvement over 2170 cells
- **Thermal Management**: Tabless design reduces heat by 80%

Production scale: 100 GWh/year at Giga Texas, 50 GWh at Giga Berlin.
