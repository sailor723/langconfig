# Tesla Q3 2025 Marketing Campaign

Tesla's Q3 2025 marketing campaign focuses on the Cybertruck and Model 3
Highland refresh. Key strategies include:

- **Social Media**: Twitter/X engagement up 45% YoY
- **Referral Program**: 500k new referrals, 12% conversion rate
- **Test Drive Events**: 250k test drives across 80 US cities
- **Digital Ads**: 12M impressions, 3.2% CTR

Budget allocation: $45M total, 60% digital, 25% events, 15% traditional.
